Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Share2Save does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

  • No account required. Share2Save works without registration or sign-in.
  • No tracking. The app contains no analytics, advertising SDKs, or usage tracking of any kind.
  • No data leaves your device. All downloaded files are stored locally in your app library. Nothing is sent to the developer's servers.
  • Standard network use only. Downloads use standard HTTPS (iOS URLSession / Background URL Session). No metadata or identifiers are transmitted beyond what iOS requires for the connection.
  • Your responsibility. The app displays a compliance notice reminding you to only download content you have the right to access.
