OFFGRID MEDIA — PRIVACY POLICY
Last updated: 1 August 2026

Offgrid Media does not collect, store or transmit any personal data.

- No account. The app works without registration or sign-in, and there is no
  backend service of any kind.
- No data collected. The App Store privacy manifest declares no tracking and no
  collected data types. The app contains no analytics, crash reporting,
  advertising or other third-party SDKs.
- Everything stays on the device. Imported media is written to a folder on the
  device (selectable in Settings) and is never uploaded. There is no cloud sync
  and no iCloud sync performed by the app.
- Network use. Playback never uses the network. The app only makes network
  requests when the user adds media from a link: it then contacts that address
  and the media server it resolves to over HTTPS. Nothing is sent to the
  developer.
- Permissions. Notifications only, so the app can report that an import
  finished or failed. Importing from Photos uses the standard iOS photo picker,
  which runs out of process and needs no photo library access.
- Diagnostics. Settings contains a local activity log that never leaves the
  device; entries are deleted automatically after two days.
- Your responsibility. Please only add content you have the right to keep.
  Compliance with the terms of service of any platform and with applicable laws
  is solely your responsibility.

Note: the website lorenzmaierhofer.com uses Plausible Analytics (no cookies, no
personal data) for aggregate page views. The app itself contains no analytics.
