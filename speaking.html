BioLong · ~250 words

Lorenz Maierhofer is Senior Director and Global Lead for AI Factory, Agentic AI Products and AI Governance at Procter & Gamble, based in Geneva, Switzerland. He leads P&G's enterprise AI platform, which enables rapid development, deployment and scaling of AI solutions under responsible AI governance, and the company-wide work on policies, processes and agentic tooling for compliant AI use. Since October 2026 he also leads the Agentic Analytics transformation, reshaping how insights are generated across P&G. Lorenz joined P&G in 2007 and worked in analyst and leadership roles across Austria, Germany and Switzerland before moving into data science. He led Europe Data Science, Global Trade Investment analytics, the Global Retail DataScience transformation and the Global GenAI program, and built a Europe commercial data science organisation of 25+ data scientists and 100+ analytical team members. His work spans AdTech and media, investment optimisation, revenue growth management, trade promotion and digital commerce. Before P&G he founded and ran Logic Mind Guide, an innovation consultancy in Graz. He holds a Dipl.-Ing. in Telematics with a major in Artificial Intelligence from Technische Universität Graz (with distinction) and a Harvard Business School Online certificate in Disruptive Strategy. In his own time he builds iOS apps end to end with AI agents.
